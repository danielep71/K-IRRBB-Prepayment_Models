## 🚦 Evidence gates and exit criteria

1. **Specification:** the [workbook contract](../WORKBOOK_CONTRACT.md), [public API register](../PUBLIC_API.txt) and methodology decisions are accepted and mutually consistent. Resolve outstanding review decisions explicitly; do not silently treat a prototype convention as accepted.
2. **Implementation:** code follows the core/facade/adapter boundaries and VBA house style. Implement the agreed operations and resolve the remaining #42 setup/capacity findings. Every built case must have an actual test; a registered placeholder is not delivered coverage.
3. **Independent reference:** #41 supplies committed synthetic inputs, independently derived expected results, tolerances and executed reference checks. Preserve the distinction between Built (Python), Built (VBA) and registered-only cases in the [case register](../methodology/TEST_CASES.md).
4. **Real Excel:** execute the required stages of [Excel evidence](../EXCEL_EVIDENCE.md) on the exact candidate SHA and record host version, bitness, references, compilation, normal and injected runs, and component round-trip comparison. Windows 64-bit evidence is required; Windows 32-bit remains best effort under that policy. NOT_RUN and UNAVAILABLE are not passes.
5. **Closeout:** all accepted milestone requirements have linked evidence, applicable integrity checks pass on the final candidate, review findings are resolved, and remaining gaps have explicit dispositions. Moving or deferring scope requires my recorded decision; this guide does not approve a deferral.

#42 and #43 can progress together: builder tooling enables the host run, while final export/compare checks the resulting candidate. They are not a requirement to close each issue before work can start on the other.

## 🗺️ Roadmap and sequencing

Within the approved milestone: establish the guide and contracts; verify regulatory assumptions; complete independent references and the corresponding implementation; assemble and exercise the candidate in Excel; complete round-trip checks, example evidence and closeout. Authoring, tooling and independent work may overlap where their inputs are already accepted.

No later milestone version, delivery date or release is committed by this guide. Any longer roadmap needs explicitly approved scope and tracked issues. The live milestone and issue dates remain planning records, not evidence of completion or release authorization.

## 🚫 Out of scope

Bank/client data, production deployment, bank-wide regulatory compliance claims, numerical certification without independent and host evidence, wholesale acceptance of #19, and unapproved future features or releases are excluded. Use synthetic examples and preserve provenance.

Before any separately approved release, assemble the exact source SHA, final-head review and CI links, accepted decision records, independent-reference results, Windows Excel evidence, build/export/component manifest comparison, packaging exclusions and known limitations.
