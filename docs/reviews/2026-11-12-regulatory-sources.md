## Corrections made

- The repricing-gap bucket table is `REG-1` Table 3 (SRP31.96), not Table 1.
- The standardised multipliers cite `REG-1` SRP31.119 and `REG-4` Article 9(3), and are no longer described as reported by the prototype.
- Decision M14 records what `REG-3` Article 3(5) requires for an exclusion of margins, instead of waiting for verification.
- The overlay records that the multipliers are applied to the toolkit's projection, not through the standardised-approach bucket allocation.
- `REG-4` is identified as Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/857.
