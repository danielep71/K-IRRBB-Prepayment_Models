# Code of conduct

Treat people respectfully. Challenge ideas with evidence and state uncertainty. Welcome different backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, retaliation, spam and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not fabricate results, misrepresent authorship or pressure anyone to disclose confidential information.

Use synthetic examples. Do not publish personal/client/employer data, credentials, proprietary methods or material you cannot share.

Report concerns privately to **danielep71@gmail.com** with relevant context and only necessary evidence. The maintainer handles reports as fairly and discreetly as practical and may warn, restrict participation or remove content proportionately. Good-faith reports are welcome; no response-time guarantee is implied.

This applies to project source, documentation, issues, PRs, reviews and related communications, including the maintainer. Vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
